About

Hi, I'm Scott.

I'm a first-year Computer Science student at Anne Arundel Community College, based in Pasadena, Maryland. I work with small businesses and startups that need a website that actually brings in customers — clean, fast, and easy to find.

Rather than handing you a generic template, I build every site around how your business actually works: what customers ask you, how they find you, and what makes them call or walk in the door.
